### Overview
This project analyzes five seasons of English Premier League (EPL) data to uncover performance patterns, trends in goal scoring, and consistency across teams. The analysis combines **web scraping**, **statistical testing**, and **correlation analysis** to evaluate team performance, goal-scoring behavior, and season-over-season dominance.

### Highlights
- Scraped EPL match data across five seasons using Python and BeautifulSoup.  
- Performed **hypothesis testing** to compare team performance statistically across seasons.  
- Conducted **correlation analysis** to understand relationships between goals scored, goals conceded, wins, and overall points.  
- Identified **Manchester City’s sustained dominance** and **Arsenal’s resurgence** through multi-year performance metrics.  
- Determined the club with the **highest average goals scored per season**, evaluating offensive efficiency across teams.  
- Highlighted teams consistently outperforming expectations based on **win percentage** and goal differential.

### Tools & Methods
- **Python:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn  
- **Web Scraping:** BeautifulSoup, Requests  
- **Statistics:** Hypothesis testing, correlation matrices  
- **Data Cleaning & Aggregation**

### Key Insights
- Manchester City emerged as the most dominant club over the five-year span.  
- Arsenal showed a high upward performance trajectory in recent seasons.  
- Goal-scoring metrics strongly correlated with total points and win percentages.  
- A small subset of teams consistently surpassed expected performance metrics based on historical data.
